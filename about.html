About Chauhan & Co.

Built by people who
show up to the site.

We're a Jaipur-based general contractor — residential, commercial, renovation and design-build. No subcontracted project managers, no disappearing between milestones. The same crew that quotes the job runs it.

Our story

Started with one crew and a rented level.

Chauhan & Co. opened in 2012 with three people and one residential contract in Vaishali Nagar. Fourteen years on, we're still run the same way: a small number of site leads who stay on a project from the first survey stake to the final punch list, instead of handing clients off between departments.

That's kept us smaller than some of the firms we compete against — and it's the reason our repeat-client rate is higher than we'd get any other way. People hire us again because the person who answered the phone during the quote is the same person who walked the final inspection.

We work across four disciplines — residential, commercial, renovation and design-build — but every job runs through the same five-stage process, whether it's an 18-unit residential row or a 140,000 sq ft office park.

How we work

What doesn't change
from job to job

Show up daily

A site lead is on-location every working day, not checking in by phone from another job.

Price it straight

One written estimate, no change orders you weren't shown coming.

Document everything

Weekly photos, a schedule you can check against, and paperwork handed over — not filed away.

Finish clean

Punch list closed before handover, not treated as a follow-up visit.
